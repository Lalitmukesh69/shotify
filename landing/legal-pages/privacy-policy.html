Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Local First Architecture

shotify is built as a desktop first application. When you take a screenshot, import an image, or apply styling options, all processing is carried out exclusively on your local computer.

Zero Data Transmission

shotify does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, screenshots, or metadata. There are no remote telemetry collectors, user identifiers, tracking pixels, or third party analytics scripts embedded inside the desktop application.

System Permissions

On macOS and Windows, screen capture capabilities require operating system authorization. These permissions are utilized strictly to obtain user requested window or region frames and are never shared.

Website Policy

The shotify landing site is hosted via GitHub Pages. Standard server connection logs may be recorded by the hosting provider according to GitHub terms of service. No tracking cookies or advertising networks are used.
